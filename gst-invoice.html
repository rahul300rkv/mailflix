GST split, explained simply

Same state

CGST + SGST (Intrastate)

When buyer and seller are in the same state, the GST rate is split equally — half goes to CGST (central) and half to SGST (state).

Different state

IGST (Interstate)

When buyer and seller are in different states, the full GST rate is charged as a single IGST instead of splitting it into two.

Slabs

5%, 12%, 18%, 28%

Most goods and services fall under one of these four GST slabs depending on the category — pick the one that applies to your item.

Commonly asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST? +

CGST and SGST apply together on intrastate sales, where the buyer and seller are in the same state, each taking half the total GST rate. IGST applies on interstate sales, where the buyer and seller are in different states, and takes the full GST rate.

How is GST calculated on an invoice? +

GST is calculated by multiplying the taxable value by the applicable GST rate. For intrastate sales this tax is split equally between CGST and SGST; for interstate sales the full rate is charged as IGST.

Can I use a cash bill instead of a GST invoice? +

A simple cash bill works for small, informal transactions, but a proper GST invoice with CGST/SGST or IGST breakup is required if you are a GST-registered business making a taxable supply.
