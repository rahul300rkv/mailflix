Everything You Need to Know About Form 12BB (Rule 26C)

In accordance with Rule 26C of the Income-tax Rules, 1962, every employee drawing salary income who wishes to claim tax deductions must submit Form 12BB to their employer. This requirement was made mandatory by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) to eliminate fraudulent deduction claims and streamline employer Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 192.

⚠️ Mandatory Landlord PAN Rule for HRA Exemption:

Under CBDT circular guidelines, if your annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 (₹8,333/month), it is legally compulsory to furnish the Permanent Account Number (PAN) of your landlord in Form 12BB. If the landlord does not possess a PAN, a signed declaration in Form 60 along with landlord address verification must be attached.

Key Deduction Categories in Form 12BB

  • 1. House Rent Allowance (Section 10(13A)): Actual rent paid, landlord name, address, and PAN. Eligible HRA is exempted based on the minimum-of-three calculation formula.
  • 2. Leave Travel Concession (Section 10(5)): Exemption for domestic travel expenses incurred by the employee and family for up to 2 journeys in a 4-year block.
  • 3. Home Loan Interest (Section 24(b)): Up to ₹2,00,000 deduction on interest paid towards purchase or construction of self-occupied residential property.
  • 4. Chapter VI-A Deductions: Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 Lakh), Section 80CCD(1B) (additional ₹50,000 for NPS), Section 80D (Health insurance premium up to ₹25k/₹50k/₹100k), and Section 80E (interest on education loans without upper ceiling).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When should I submit Form 12BB to my company?
Most Indian employers (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Wipro, banks, startups) open their payroll investment verification window in January or early February. Form 12BB must be submitted along with physical or digital proof of payments (ELSS statement, LIC premium receipt, home loan interest certificate, rent receipts) before the cutoff date so HR can factor them into your final February and March payslips.
Is Form 12BB required under the New Tax Regime?
If you have opted for the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D), HRA, and Section 24b home loan interest are not allowed. However, employer contribution to NPS under Section 80CCD(2) and Standard Deduction (₹75,000 in FY 2025-26) are automatically applied by payroll without needing Form 12BB proofs.
What if I cannot arrange the landlord's PAN card?
If your annual rent is greater than ₹1,00,000 and your landlord refuses to provide their PAN, your employer cannot legally grant HRA exemption in your Form 16, resulting in higher TDS. However, you can still claim HRA directly while filing your annual Income Tax Return (ITR), though the tax department may flag it for scrutiny.
