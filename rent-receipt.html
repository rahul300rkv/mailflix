Commonly asked questions

Is a rent receipt enough to claim HRA exemption? +

Yes, monthly rent receipts are the standard proof employers ask for to process HRA exemption in payroll. If your annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000, you also need the landlord's PAN on the receipt.

Do I need a revenue stamp on a rent receipt? +

A Re. 1 revenue stamp is required on rent receipts when cash payment for a single receipt exceeds ₹5,000. It is generally not required for bank transfers, though some employers still ask for it as a formality.

What details must a rent receipt have? +

A valid rent receipt should include tenant name, landlord name, rent amount in figures and words, property address, rent period, date, and the landlord's signature. PAN is needed if annual rent crosses ₹1,00,000.

Can I generate 12 months of rent receipts at once? +

This tool generates one receipt at a time so each month's date and receipt number stay accurate. Simply change the month and date fields and generate again for each month you need.
