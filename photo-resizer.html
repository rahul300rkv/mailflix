Why portals reject your upload

File size

Wrong KB, not wrong photo

Most "invalid image" errors are about file size, not quality. A great photo at 200KB still gets rejected if the portal wants under 50KB.

Dimensions

Exact aspect ratio matters

Passport photos need 3.5:4.5 cm proportions specifically. A square or wrongly-cropped photo gets auto-rejected even if the size is fine.

Format

JPEG over PNG

Government portals almost always expect JPEG. PNGs are often larger for the same visual quality and can fail upload validation.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser

This tool uses HTML canvas to resize and compress locally. Your photo, signature or ID document is never sent to any server.

Commonly asked questions

What is the photo size limit for Passport Seva? +

Passport Seva requires a photo of 3.5 × 4.5 cm, usually between 20KB and 50KB in JPEG format. This tool resizes and compresses your photo to fit that exact range automatically.

What is the signature size limit for govt portals? +

Most Indian government portals, including EPFO and PSC exam forms, require a signature image around 3.5 × 1.5 cm and under 10KB to 20KB. This tool compresses your signature to meet that limit.

Is it safe to upload my photo or signature here? +

Yes. All resizing and compression happens entirely inside your browser using canvas processing. Your image is never uploaded to any server, so your identity documents stay private.

Why does my portal reject my photo even after resizing? +

Most rejections happen because the file size, not just the dimensions, is outside the allowed KB range. This tool automatically reduces JPEG quality step by step until the file fits the target size.
