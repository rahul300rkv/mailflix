How it works

Type or paste your text into the left box — up to 2,000 characters. Choose a rewriting mode and your preferred output language, then click Paraphrase. The AI rewrites the text while preserving the original meaning, and the result appears on the right. Copy it, tweak it, and use it wherever you need it.

When is paraphrasing useful?

Emails, cover letters, WhatsApp messages, blog drafts, educational notes, professional summaries — any writing task where you want to express something differently, more clearly, or in a different language. The Formal and Fluency modes are especially useful before sending anything official.

Paraphrasing vs summarizing

Paraphrasing rewrites the original content using different words while keeping all the important details. Summarizing shortens the content and focuses only on the main points. This tool paraphrases — it does not shrink your content.

Frequently asked questions

Is this paraphrasing tool free? +

Yes, completely free with no signup required. A fair-use limit of 20 paraphrases per hour per connection applies to keep the service available for everyone.

Can I paraphrase Hindi text? +

Yes. Select Hindi as the output language and the tool will rewrite your text in Hindi, preserving the original meaning.

Can I paraphrase Hinglish? +

Yes. Select Hinglish when you want natural Hindi-English mixed-language output — the kind of language used in everyday Indian conversations and messages.

Does the tool change the meaning? +

It is designed to preserve the original meaning, but AI-generated text can occasionally introduce errors. Always review the result before using it in anything important.

Is paraphrasing the same as copying? +

No. Paraphrasing changes the wording and structure while retaining the underlying idea. When using someone else's ideas or information, follow the appropriate citation and attribution requirements for your context.
