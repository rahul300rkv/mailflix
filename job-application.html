Frequently Asked Questions

How is an AI-generated job application email different from a template?
Templates are generic and recruiters can spot them instantly. This tool generates a unique email based on the specific job title, company name, and your background — making it feel personal and relevant. You can further edit the output to add specific achievements or tone adjustments.
Should I change anything in the generated email before sending?
Yes, always review and personalise. Add a specific reason why you want to work at that company, include a recent achievement with numbers if possible (e.g. 'grew sales by 30%'), and adjust the tone to match the company culture. The AI gives you a strong first draft, not a final send.
What should a good job application email include?
A strong application email includes: a clear subject line with the job title and your name, a brief introduction and why you're applying, 2-3 relevant skills or experiences, a specific achievement or value you bring, and a professional closing with your contact details and CV attachment.
Can I use this for fresher job applications?
Absolutely. The tool adapts to your profile — if you mention you're a fresher or recent graduate, it focuses on academic achievements, internships, skills from college projects, and your enthusiasm to learn rather than extensive work experience.
How do I make my application stand out to recruiters in India?
Reference something specific about the company (recent news, product launch, their mission). Mention skills that appear in the job description. Keep it under 250 words — Indian recruiters receive hundreds of applications and value concise, clear emails. Attach a clean, ATS-friendly resume.
