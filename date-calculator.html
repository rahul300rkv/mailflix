Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated? +

The calculator finds the difference between your date of birth and the target date in complete years, then months, then days — accounting for different month lengths and leap years. If the day of the month hasn't been reached yet in the current month, it borrows from the previous month's actual day count.

Does the age calculator handle leap years? +

Yes. The calculation uses actual calendar month lengths and handles leap years correctly, so someone born on 29 February gets an accurate result for any target date.

How many days between two dates? +

Enter a start date and end date. The calculator uses UTC timestamps to avoid daylight saving issues and returns the exact number of calendar days, complete weeks, and remaining days.

Why would I need to know days between dates? +

Common uses include calculating notice period duration for resignation, checking how many days of service you have completed for gratuity or leave encashment, finding deadline gaps, or verifying age for government forms and job applications.
