Professional Tax in India — State-wise Rates 2025-26

Updated August 2026 · Covers all states that levy professional tax

Professional Tax (PT) is a state-level tax on employment income, levied by state governments under Article 276 of the Indian Constitution. The maximum professional tax any state can levy is ₹2,500 per year. It is deducted monthly by your employer from your salary and deposited with the state government.

Key facts: Professional tax is deductible from your income under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act — it reduces your taxable income. It applies to salaried employees, self-employed professionals, freelancers, and in some states, businesses. Not all states levy PT.

States That DO NOT Have Professional Tax

Employees in these states pay zero professional tax: Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, Punjab, Bihar, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.

Maharashtra has one of the highest professional tax rates in India, up to ₹2,500 per year.

Odisha, Assam, Meghalaya & Other States

Odisha: Up to ₹1,000 annual PT. Slabs start from ₹1.67L annual salary (₹13,900/month). Maximum ₹2,500/year for income above ₹20,000/month.

Assam: PT up to ₹208/month. Applies for monthly salary above ₹10,000.

Meghalaya: PT up to ₹2,500/year. Slabs start at ₹4,167/month income.

Goa: PT up to ₹2,500/year. Applies on gross monthly salary above ₹15,000.

Is Professional Tax Deductible from Income Tax?

Yes. Professional tax paid during the year is fully deductible from taxable income under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act. This applies under both New and Old Tax Regimes. So if you pay ₹2,400/year in PT, your taxable income is reduced by ₹2,400 before computing income tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Indian states have professional tax?
States that levy professional tax include Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Gujarat, Kerala, Odisha, Assam, Meghalaya, and Goa. States with no professional tax include Delhi, UP, Rajasthan, Haryana, MP, Bihar, and most northeastern states.
What is the maximum professional tax in India?
The maximum professional tax any state can levy is ₹2,500 per year (₹2,400 in some states). This limit is set by Article 276 of the Indian Constitution, which restricts states from levying profession tax above ₹2,500 annually. Maharashtra charges ₹2,500 (₹200×11 + ₹300 in February).
Is professional tax deductible from income tax?
Yes. Professional tax paid is deductible from your gross salary income under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act. This deduction is available under both New and Old Tax Regimes. If you pay ₹2,400 in professional tax annually, your taxable salary is reduced by ₹2,400 before computing income tax.
Why is February professional tax higher in Maharashtra?
In Maharashtra, professional tax is ₹200 per month for 11 months (April to January) and ₹300 in February — making the annual total ₹2,500. The higher February deduction is a regulatory quirk unique to Maharashtra's Professional Tax Act to ensure the annual cap of ₹2,500 is collected.
Is professional tax applicable for women employees?
In Maharashtra, women earning up to ₹25,000/month are exempt from professional tax. Other states like Karnataka and Tamil Nadu do not offer gender-based exemptions — professional tax applies to all employees based on salary slabs regardless of gender.
