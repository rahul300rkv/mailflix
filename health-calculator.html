All 7 health calculators explained

BMI

Body Mass Index

Weight ÷ height² — a screening measure for weight categories. Doesn't directly measure body fat.

BMR

Basal Metabolic Rate

Calories your body burns at complete rest using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. Starting point for calorie planning.

TDEE

Daily Calories

BMR × activity multiplier — your total daily energy expenditure. Shows loss, maintenance and gain targets.

Ideal Weight

Devine Formula

Estimated ideal body weight from height alone. An estimate — not a medical target.

Body Fat

U.S. Navy Method

Estimates body fat from neck, waist and hip measurements. More accessible than calipers but less precise than DEXA.

Water

Daily Hydration

General rule of ~35 ml per kg. Actual needs vary with climate, exercise and diet.

Macros

Macronutrient Grams

Converts a calorie target and %split into grams of protein (4 kcal/g), carbs (4 kcal/g) and fat (9 kcal/g).

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI range? +

The standard BMI ranges are: below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 and above is obese. BMI is a screening tool, not a direct measure of health or body fat.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE? +

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the calories your body uses at complete rest — just to keep organs functioning. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) multiplies BMR by your activity level to give your actual daily calorie needs.

How accurate is the body fat calculator? +

The U.S. Navy circumference method gives a reasonable estimate but can be off by several percentage points compared to professional methods like DEXA scans or hydrostatic weighing. Use it as a directional guide, not a precise measurement.

How much water should I drink per day? +

A common estimate is 35 ml per kg of body weight. A 70 kg person would need about 2.45 litres — roughly 10 glasses of water. This varies with exercise, climate, diet and individual health.
