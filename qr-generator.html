What you can encode in a QR code

Website

URLs & landing pages

Paste any website URL — print the QR on visiting cards, flyers, or product packaging to let people scan and open your site instantly.

Payments

UPI IDs & payment links

Encode your UPI ID or a payment link — customers can scan and pay without typing anything. Works with PhonePe, GPay, Paytm and any UPI app.

Contact

Phone, email or vCard

Encode a phone number (tel:+91XXXXXXXXXX) or a vCard string so people can save your contact directly from a scan.

Any text

Plain text, WiFi, social links

Encode WiFi credentials, Instagram handles, WhatsApp links, or any plain text message — there's no restriction on what goes inside a QR code.
