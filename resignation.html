Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard notice period in India?
Most private sector jobs in India have a 30-day or 60-day notice period as specified in the offer letter. IT and senior roles often have 90 days. Government jobs may have 30-90 days depending on the department. Check your offer letter for the exact notice period before setting your last working day.
Can I get an early release from my notice period?
Yes, but it requires mutual agreement with your employer. You can negotiate early release by completing critical handovers, finding/training a replacement, or offering a buy-out of remaining notice days. The 'HR Nudge' template in this tool is specifically crafted to request early release professionally.
Do I need to give a reason in my resignation letter?
No, you are not legally required to state a reason for resignation in India. Keeping the letter neutral and professional ('personal reasons' or 'to pursue other opportunities') is generally recommended to maintain a good relationship with your employer.
Should I resign verbally first or submit the letter directly?
Best practice is to inform your direct manager verbally (in-person or on a call) before sending the formal resignation letter. This shows respect and gives them time to process before the formal record. Inform HR on the same day or after your manager conversation.
Can I withdraw a resignation after submitting it?
Yes, in most cases — unless your employer has already accepted the resignation in writing and started replacement processes. A verbal or email request to withdraw before the last working day is usually possible. There is no automatic legal right to withdrawal, so it depends on your employer's discretion.
