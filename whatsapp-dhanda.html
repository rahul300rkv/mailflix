Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hinglish and why does it work for WhatsApp business?
Hinglish is the natural blend of Hindi and English that most urban and semi-urban Indians speak daily. WhatsApp business messages in Hinglish feel personal and relatable rather than corporate. Studies show Hinglish messages get 2-3x higher reply rates compared to formal English-only texts in the Indian market.
What kinds of businesses can use this tool?
Any small or medium business that sells via WhatsApp — kirana stores, clothing boutiques, tiffin services, tuition centres, beauty parlours, freelancers, travel agents, insurance agents, real estate brokers, and online resellers. The tool generates messages for B2C (customer-facing) and B2B (business-to-business) communication.
How do I avoid my WhatsApp account getting banned for business messages?
Use WhatsApp Business app (not the personal app) for bulk messaging. Avoid sending to people who haven't opted in. Use personalised messages — not identical bulk blasts. Space out messages across time. Avoid suspicious links. For high-volume messaging, use WhatsApp Business API through official providers like Interakt, Wati, or AiSensy.
Can I generate messages for festivals and seasonal offers?
Yes. The tool supports seasonal message templates for Diwali, Eid, Holi, New Year, Independence Day, and other Indian festivals. Festive messages with a subtle business offer (not hard sell) perform best during festival periods.
How long should a WhatsApp business message be?
Keep it under 150 words — ideally 3-5 lines. WhatsApp chats are personal spaces, and long messages feel like spam. Lead with the most important information (offer, price, or benefit), use 1-2 relevant emojis, and end with a clear CTA like 'Reply INTERESTED' or 'Call/WhatsApp us at [number]'.
