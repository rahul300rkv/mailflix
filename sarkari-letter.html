Frequently Asked Questions

What types of government letters can I generate?
The tool generates applications for: water/electricity/municipal complaints (Bijli-Paani shikayat), police complaints (FIR request, lost property), bank applications (account opening, loan NOC, name change), RTI (Right to Information) applications, and general applications to government departments.
Should I write government letters in Hindi or English?
State government offices (especially in Hindi-belt states like UP, MP, Rajasthan, Bihar) generally prefer Hindi. Central government offices and urban municipal corporations accept English. Banks accept both. When in doubt, submit one copy in each language — this tool supports both.
What is the correct format for a government application letter?
A standard government letter format includes: your name and address (top left), date, the designation and address of the recipient, a clear subject line starting with 'Vishay:' or 'Subject:', the body of the letter (formal, factual, numbered points), and closing with 'Bhavdeey' (Hindi) or 'Yours faithfully' (English) followed by your signature.
How do I file an RTI application?
An RTI (Right to Information Act 2005) application is filed to the Public Information Officer (PIO) of the concerned department with a ₹10 fee (or online on the RTI portal for Central Government). The authority must respond within 30 days. Use the RTI template in this tool to draft a correctly formatted request.
Can I use this for bank-related official letters?
Yes. The tool generates bank letters for: savings account opening, change of address/name/nominee, account closure, loan NOC requests, fixed deposit premature closure, and NEFT/RTGS dispute letters — all in the formal language banks expect.
